CareFlow is an app that deals with health, so any mistake or delay in our notification could have more consequences than a non-health related automation. There are a couple of issues that could occur if our system is down, like reminders not arriving or AI incorrectly classifying a message as worrying. CareFlow should be trusted for things like medical reminders, daily check-ins or patterns in its patients responses, but I wouldn't let it make medical decisions on its own. 

For the average person, CareFlow can still be useful by sending the medication reminders, keep track of how users are feeling and involve others whenever necessary. However, the system is not foolproof and should be used as a support system alongside humans, professionals or a local doctor/practitioner, not as a complete replacement.

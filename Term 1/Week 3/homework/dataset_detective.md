# Dataset detective - who is missing?

**The dataset:** Population per district (*bevolking per wijk*) of The Hague, on [Den Haag in Cijfers](https://denhaag.incijfers.nl), the open statistics website of the municipality.

**Where the numbers come from:** the BRP (*Basisregistratie Personen*), the Dutch population register. Someone is only counted if they are registered at an address in The Hague.

**Who or what is missing?**

- **People who stay less than 4 months**, for example seasonal and labour migrants or exchange students. They do not register in the BRP but in the separate RNI register, so they are not in the district numbers, even though they live and work in the city.
- **People without a fixed address**, such as homeless people. They can only be registered through a postal address, so the dataset does not show in which district they actually sleep or need help.
- **People who are not registered at all**, like undocumented migrants. They are completely invisible in this data.
- **People registered at a different address than where they live**, for example students who are still registered at their parents' home. They are counted in the wrong place.

**Why it matters:** if the city plans services (health care, food banks, housing) per district based on this dataset, the groups above are counted as zero. It is the same problem as Ypenburg in Exercise 3, where missing data turns into "nobody lives here". Often these are the people who need the services the most.

# Resit - Workshop Week 4: Text, strings & files

I missed the Week 4 workshop (strings, bag of words, reading and writing files).
This folder shows that I completed the in-class assignments and the homework.

## In-class assignments
All in [Week4_Workshop_Student.ipynb](Week4_Workshop_Student.ipynb):
- Wave 1 - The string toolkit: cleaning one piece of citizen feedback.
- Wave 2 - From text to numbers: word frequency with a bag of words
  and stopwords.
- Wave 3 - Reading and writing files: write a feedback file, read it back,
  flag urgent lines and write a report.
- Reflections: whose feedback is in the file, and who decides what is urgent?
- Big individual assignment - Community Feedback Analyzer. Also as a
  separate script: [feedback_analyzer.py](feedback_analyzer.py)
  (run with `python feedback_analyzer.py`), tested on
  [community_feedback.txt](community_feedback.txt) ->
  [feedback_report.txt](feedback_report.txt).
